# JKK Wiki

Welcome to the JKK wiki! Here you can find information about our research, projects, and publications.

![Image](images_common/aw_sze05.png){ width="80%" }



Technical documentation for JKK's autonomous vehicle research platforms (hardware specs, our ROS 2 software stack, and datasets) covering the vehicles, robots, and test infrastructure at Győr Campus and ZalaZone. Written for lab members, students, and research partners working directly with our systems.

Looking for our research mission or publication list instead? See [Home](https://jkk-research.github.io/latest/) or [Publications](https://jkk-research.github.io/latest/papers/).

**Hardware**

Sensor and vehicle specifications for our research platforms: ground vehicle sensor sets, GNSS/LiDAR/radar/camera/IMU datasheets, and actuation (PACMod, LabVIEW).


**Software**

Our ROS 2 stack: sensor drivers, networking/QoS, localization, Autoware integration, HD maps, and lab utilities.


**Datasets**

Recorded rosbag/mcap logs and HLB use-case data from our vehicles, released for research and teaching.

[Full dataset catalog](https://jkk-research.github.io/dataset/){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
