# Luminar Iris

## Features

- Long-range perception for highway autonomous driving
- Eye-safe infrared wavelength (1550 nm)
- Digital lidar technology for improved reliability
- High-resolution 3D perception
- Integrated signal processing

## ROS 2 Driver

[luminar-ros](https://github.com/jkk-research/luminar_ros2_driver) - ROS 2 driver for Luminar Iris
