| Spec | ZED 2i | ZED 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | 120 mm | 120 mm |
| Max video modes | 2K@15, 1080p@30, 720p@60, 672x376 up to 100 FPS | 2K@15, 1080p@30, 720p@60, 672x376 up to 100 FPS |
| Shutter | Synchronized rolling shutter | Synchronized rolling shutter |
| Interface | USB Type-C with dual locking screws | USB Type-C |
| Depth range (typical) | 0.3-20 m (2.1 mm) or 1.5-35 m (4 mm) | Shorter practical range than 2i (model dependent) |
| Lens options | 2.1 mm or 4 mm, optional CPL | More limited optics options |
| Sensors | IMU + barometer + magnetometer + temperature | IMU-centric setup |
| Ruggedization | IP66 | Lower environmental protection than 2i |

## Choose fast

- Choose ZED 2i for outdoor robots, harsh environments, and flexible optics.
- Choose ZED 2 when you already have a working legacy pipeline and do not need IP66 or lens options.
